What is Claude Duet?

Claude Duet is a proactive agent for work from Anthropic, currently in research preview. Text it a task and it comes back with next steps. It also notices what needs doing, handles it in the background, and reaches out when it needs you.

Good to know before you try it

  • It is a research preview, so access and features may differ from what you see here.
  • Claude Duet counts toward your Claude usage limits.
  • It reads your connected apps and can act in them on its own, and it can make mistakes, so check its work.
  • You can turn off any connected app in Settings.
