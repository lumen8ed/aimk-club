The big idea

A Robot With 5 Labeled Boxes

Think of your AI Agent as a little robot with 5 labeled boxes. It sorts your messy files into those boxes, and it never throws anything away.

Your messy filesHand them to the robot.
Your robotReads each file and picks a box.
Your 5 boxesEvery file ends up with a home.
How it works

4 Simple Steps

Step 1

Hand It Over

Give the robot the files you want sorted.

Step 2

Watch It Sort

The robot puts each file into one of your five boxes.

Step 3

Check the Odd Ones

When the robot is unsure where a file goes, it uses the unsure box so you can take a look yourself.

Step 4

Read the Report

The robot tells you what happened and gives you one next step.
